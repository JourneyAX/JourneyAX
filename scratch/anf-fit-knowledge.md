## Men's Tops Size Chart & Fit Guide
type: sizing

Use this to answer "what size top am I at Abercrombie?" for men. A&F men's tops run in XS–XXL, and today they fit true to size (A&F historically ran slim, but current tops are cut fuller than the old reputation). Approximate chest and neck ranges (a guide, verify against the on-page chart):

- XS: chest 34–36 in (86–91 cm), neck ~31.5–32 in
- S: chest 36–38 in (91–96 cm), neck 32.5–33.5 in
- M: chest 38–40 in (96–102 cm), neck 34–34.5 in
- L: chest 40–42 in (102–107 cm), neck 35–35.5 in
- XL: chest 42–44 in (107–112 cm), neck 36–36.5 in
- XXL: chest 44–46 in (112–117 cm), neck 37–37.5 in

Men's fits to know: **Slim** (trim through chest and body, tapered — closest to the classic A&F look), **Muscle** (fitted through chest, shoulders and biceps for a built frame), **Classic/Relaxed** (straighter, roomier body — the most forgiving), and **Oversized** (deliberately baggy, drops off the shoulder). If you lift or have a broad chest, Muscle or size up in Slim. Between sizes on a Slim tee? Size up for comfort or stay true for a fitted look. Oversized already includes extra room, so take your normal size.

## Men's Jeans Size & Fit Guide
type: sizing

A&F men's jeans use waist-inch sizing, roughly 28–40 (commonly 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 36, 38), with inseam lengths Short/Regular/Long ≈ 30" / 32" / 34". Buy your true waist measurement in inches; most washes use A&F's Vintage Stretch so they give a little. Rigid (non-stretch) styles run firmer — see the denim care note.

Named A&F men's jean fits and who each suits:
- **Skinny** — tightest through thigh and ankle; slim builds, fashion-forward.
- **Slim** — fitted but not sprayed on; the versatile all-rounder for average builds.
- **Athletic Slim** — extra room in thigh and seat, tapered leg; muscular legs who still want a slim look.
- **Slim Straight** — slim seat, straight (not tapered) leg; wants slim without ankle-hugging.
- **Athletic Straight** — roomy thigh/seat, straight leg; big quads, relaxed vibe.
- **Straight** — classic even leg top to bottom; safe, timeless.
- **90s Straight** — ultra-high ~11.5" rise, eases into a straight leg; retro, tucked-in styling.
- **90s Relaxed** — high rise, easy through thigh; trend-driven, comfortable.
- **Loose** — generous through hip and leg; streetwear, comfort-first.
- **Baggy / Ultra Baggy** — lowest rise, widest leg; statement, very relaxed.
- **Bootcut** — slim-to-knee then flares over boots; taller frames, western lean.

Between sizes? Stretch fits: size down. Rigid or loose/baggy fits: take true waist.

## Women's Tops Size Chart
type: sizing

For "what size top am I?" A&F women's tops come in both letter (XS–XXL) and numeric (00–16) sizing depending on style. Fitted tops, bodysuits and going-out styles run snug — if you're between sizes or fuller in the bust, size up. Relaxed tees, sweatshirts and oversized shirts are forgiving; take your normal size or size down for a closer fit.

Approximate bust and waist by size (a guide — confirm on the product's chart):

- 00 / XS: bust 32–33 in (81–84 cm), waist 24–25 in

## Women's Jeans Size & Fit Guide
type: sizing

A&F women's jeans use numeric sizing (00–16) that maps closely to waist inches: 00≈24", 0≈25", 2≈26", 4≈27", 6≈28", 8≈29", 10≈30", 12≈31", 14≈32", 16≈33". Buy your natural-waist inch measurement. Inseams come in **Short / Regular / Long / Extra-Long** (roughly 26–27" / 29–30" / 31–32" / 33"+), so match length to height.

Rises: **Mid Rise** (sits below the belly button), **High Rise** (~10.5", at the natural waist), **Ultra High Rise** (~11.5", tucks and elongates the leg).

**Curve Love** is A&F's signature: the same style cut with ~2" extra room through the hip and thigh to stop waist gap on hourglass and curvier figures — no gapping waistband while still fitting the seat. If you constantly get waist gap, choose Curve Love; if you're straighter through the hip, the classic fit is cleaner.

Named women's fits: **Mom** (relaxed, tapered, vintage high rise), **90s** (straight, ultra-high, roomy retro), **Dad** (loose, slouchy, gender-neutral ease), **Baggy** (widest, low-to-mid rise), **Ankle Straight** (straight leg cropped at the ankle), **Flare** (fitted then dramatic flare), **Bootcut** (slim to knee, gentle flare), **Skinny** (tight, tapered). Between sizes in stretch denim: size down.

## Does A&F Run True to Size?
type: fit

Short answer: **yes, A&F now runs largely true to size.** The brand historically ran slim (the early-2000s reputation), but current cuts are graded truer. Nuance by category:

- **Men's & women's relaxed tees, sweatshirts, oversized shirts, wide-leg pants** — true to size or forgiving. Take your normal size; size down only if you want a closer fit.
- **Fitted tops, bodysuits, going-out tops, dresses (women's)** — run snug. If you're between sizes, fuller in the bust/hip, or want comfort, **size up**.
- **Stretch jeans (both)** — often best sized **down** because Vintage Stretch relaxes with wear; a snug first hour is normal.
- **Rigid / non-stretch and loose/baggy jeans** — take your **true waist**; they won't give.
- **Men's Slim & Muscle tops** — trim; broad chests or lifters size up or pick Classic/Muscle.

"Between sizes" default: for structured or fitted pieces size up; for stretch denim size down; for relaxed knits stay true. Always sanity-check the specific product's reviews and on-page size chart, since fit varies by fabric and style.

## How to Measure Yourself
type: measurement

To get the right size, measure with a soft tape over light clothing, tape snug but not tight, and stand relaxed. Answer "how do I measure for jeans / tops / dresses" with these:

- **Chest / Bust** — wrap the tape around the fullest part of the chest (men) or bust (women), level under the arms and across the shoulder blades. Keep it parallel to the floor. This sizes tops, jackets and dresses.
- **Natural waist** — the narrowest part of the torso, usually just above the belly button. Don't suck in. This sizes jeans, skirts and dress waists. A&F numeric jean sizes roughly equal your waist inches.
- **Hips / seat** — the fullest part of the hips and seat, feet together. Critical for dresses and for choosing Curve Love.
- **Inseam** — from the crotch seam straight down to where you want the hem (ankle bone for full length). Or measure a well-fitting pair of jeans lying flat, inner-seam top to hem. Compare to Short/Regular/Long.

**No tape?** Use a phone charging cable or string, mark the length with your finger, then measure it against any ruler or a US dollar bill (6.14 in / 15.6 cm long). Or read the waist tag on jeans that already fit and match that inch number.

## Fabric & Care — Linen
type: care

A&F stocks a lot of linen (and linen-blend) shirts, shorts, trousers and dresses for summer. Linen is breathable, moisture-wicking and stays cool, which makes it ideal for hot-weather, beach and vacation dressing — but it wrinkles by nature and that relaxed crumple is part of the look, not a defect. Fit-wise, linen has almost no stretch, so if a linen button-up or trouser is between sizes, size up for ease of movement.

Care: pure linen can shrink up to ~5–10% the first wash, so wash cold or lukewarm on a gentle cycle and reshape while damp; for tailored linen pieces, dry-clean or hand-wash to hold the fit. Air-dry flat or on a hanger rather than a hot tumble, which shrinks and stiffens the weave. Iron while slightly damp on a medium-hot steam setting, or lean into the natural texture. Linen softens and drapes better with every wash. Store folded, not crushed, and it'll be your coolest, most comfortable warm-weather layer.

## Fabric & Care — Denim / Jeans
type: care

A&F denim spans several washes: **light** (casual, summery, shows fading), **medium** (the versatile everyday wash), **dark** (dressier, slimming, easiest to dress up), **black** (sleek, night-out), and **rinse** (deep near-black raw indigo, most formal). Most A&F jeans are **stretch** (Vintage Stretch) — comfortable, they mold to you and may relax up to half a size, which is why sizing down often works. **Rigid / non-stretch** denim holds its shape and should be bought at true waist; expect a firmer break-in.

Care to protect fit and color: wash jeans **inside out, cold, gently, and infrequently** — denim doesn't need frequent washing and heat is the enemy. Skip the dryer or use low/air; high heat shrinks cotton and can pull a stretch jean a size smaller (usually recoverable after wear). Dark and black washes bleed, so wash separately the first few times and avoid over-drying, which fades them. If a pair labeled shrink-to-fit or 100% cotton feels loose, a warm wash and dry will tighten it slightly. Hang or fold; re-wear several times between washes.

## Fabric & Care — Knits & Sweaters
type: care

A&F sweaters and knits (cotton, wool blends, cashmere-touch) hold their shape best with gentle handling. Wash cold on a delicate cycle or by hand, turn inside out, and use a mild detergent. Never wring or hang a wet sweater — the weight stretches the shoulders and lengthens the body. Instead, **lay flat to dry**, reshaping to size while damp. Store folded, not hung, to prevent shoulder bumps and sagging. Pilling is normal with wear; shave gently with a fabric comb or pill remover. If a knit feels slightly tight, most relax with wear; if it's loose, a cool wash and low-heat reshaping tightens the fibers. For fitted or cropped knits that run snug, size up if you're between sizes.

## Fabric & Care — Cotton Tees & Activewear
type: care

**Cotton tees:** A&F's cotton and slub tees are soft and can shrink slightly on first wash. Wash cold, inside out, and tumble low or air-dry to preserve length — hot drying is the main cause of a tee riding up or feeling tight. Graphic and printed tees should be washed inside out to protect the print. If you want a lived-in fit, a warm wash tightens them a touch.

**Activewear (YPB and similar):** these performance pieces use stretch synthetics (nylon/elastane/poly blends) designed to be snug and compressive — that's the intended fit, so buy true to size unless you want extra coverage. Wash cold, gentle, with no fabric softener (it coats fibers and kills moisture-wicking and stretch). Air-dry or tumble low; high heat degrades elastane and shortens the garment's life. Wash after each sweaty wear to prevent odor set-in.

## Fit Troubleshooting
type: fit

Quick fixes for the most common A&F fit complaints:

- **Shoulders tight but chest fits (men's tops):** you're likely in a Slim or Muscle cut built for a narrower or more athletic frame. Move to **Classic/Relaxed** fit or size up one; Muscle helps if biceps and chest are the issue but shoulders are the pinch point.
- **Waist gaps on jeans (women's):** classic sign you need **Curve Love**, which adds ~2" through the hip and thigh so the waistband sits flush on an hourglass shape. Alternatively size down if the whole jean is loose, or have the waistband taken in.
- **Top too long / hits low:** you may be in a longer-line or oversized cut — try a **cropped** or fitted style, size down, or front-tuck/half-tuck to shorten the visual line.
- **Jeans too long:** choose a **Short** inseam or a cropped/ankle style rather than hemming a flare.
- **Tall (5'10"+):** favor **Long / Extra-Long** inseams and non-cropped tops; oversized and 90s styles have extra length.
- **Petite (under 5'4"):** choose **Short** inseams, mini or ankle lengths, cropped tops, and expect to hem maxi styles. Size by measurement, not by frame.

## Complete-the-Look Logic
type: styling

To build a full A&F outfit around one hero item, work in three moves. **1) Pick the hero** — the piece the customer already loves (a linen shirt, a pair of 90s jeans, a fitted dress). **2) Balance the silhouette** — pair a fitted piece with a relaxed one and vice versa: slim top with wide-leg jeans, oversized shirt with a fitted or cropped base, a flowy dress with a structured layer. Never stack two very loose or two very tight pieces. **3) Add a layer and a shoe** so the look reads finished: a denim or linen jacket, cardigan, overshirt or blazer, plus footwear that matches the formality (sneaker → casual, loafer/boot → smart, heel/sandal → dressy).

**Color for A&F's palette:** the brand lives in neutrals (white, sand, camel, grey, black), denim blues, and earth tones (olive, rust, brown). Anchor with one or two neutrals, add at most one accent color, and let denim act as a neutral. Tonal (all-beige, all-denim) looks polished; a neutral base with a single earth-tone pop looks intentional. Keep metals and leathers consistent. This layering-and-palette logic turns any single item into a complete, on-brand journey from top to shoe.

## Stylist Clarifying Questions
type: fit

Before recommending a size and fit, the stylist should ask **no more than 3** targeted questions (per the 3-question rule) — ideally these:

1. **"What size do you usually wear, and in which brand?"** — anchors the recommendation to a known reference so we can translate to A&F's true-to-size grading (and flag if that brand runs large or small).
2. **"Do you like your fit snug/fitted or relaxed/roomy?"** — decides whether to size up (structured/fitted styles run snug), stay true (relaxed knits), or size down (stretch denim), and which named fit to steer toward.
3. **"What's the occasion — and for jeans, how tall are you?"** — occasion drives the garment and formality (date, wedding, work, weekend, vacation), and height sets the inseam length (Short/Regular/Long/Extra-Long) and rise.

If the customer is buying jeans and reports frequent waist gap, add the Curve Love recommendation without spending a separate question. Only ask what's needed — if usual size and occasion are already known from context, ask just the one remaining question, then present the plan.
