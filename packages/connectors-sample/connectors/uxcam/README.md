# UXCam (sample)

Stands in for: **UXCam: how people use the mobile app**.

Mobile app sessions, screens and where people drop out. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/uxcam`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sessions` | 42 | made up | 42/42 | - | every field |
| `screens` | 30 | made up | 30/30 | - | every field |
| `funnel_steps` | 25 | made up | 25/25 | - | every field |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): sessions 25, screens 25, funnel_steps 25.

## Tools

- `list_sessions`: App sessions, newest first, by outcome, app version or platform.
- `get_session`: One app session with the screens visited.
- `screen_stats`: How each app screen is used: views, time spent and how often people leave from it. Worst exit rate first.
- `drop_off`: The steps of a funnel, with how many people reach each and how many drop out.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
