# Salesforce Marketing Cloud (sample)

Stands in for: **Salesforce Marketing Cloud: messages to customers and the in-app inbox**.

Messages sent to trade accounts and the templates they come from. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/marketing-cloud`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `messages` | 34 | made up | 34/34 | - | every field |
| `contacts` | 30 | made up | 30/30 | - | every field |
| `templates` | 8 | made up | 8/8 | - | every field |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): messages 25, contacts 25, templates 6.

## Tools

- `list_messages`: Messages sent to an account (or all accounts), newest first.
- `get_message`: One message with its delivery status.
- `list_templates`: The message templates that can be sent.
- `send_message` (changes data, so it asks first): Queue a message to an account from a template. This sends something to a customer, so it asks first.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
