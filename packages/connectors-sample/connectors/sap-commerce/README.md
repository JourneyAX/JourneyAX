# SAP Commerce Cloud (sample)

Stands in for: **SAP Commerce Cloud (Hybris): products, carts, checkout and orders**.

Products, carts and orders of the online shop. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/sap-commerce`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `products` | 100 | real | 0/100 | every field | - |
| `carts` | 30 | made up | 30/30 | - | cartId, accountId, status, branchCode, createdAt |
| `cart_lines` | 87 | real and made up | 87/87 | sku, title, unitPrice (the real shelf price), unitOfMeasure | lineId, cartId, quantity, lineTotal |
| `orders` | 48 | made up | 48/48 | branchCode (a real code) | orderId, accountId, customerType, status, deliveryType, placedAt, lineCount, total |
| `order_lines` | 111 | real and made up | 111/111 | sku, title, unitPrice (the real shelf price), unitOfMeasure | lineId, orderId, quantity, lineTotal |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): products 40, carts 25, cart_lines 25, orders 25, order_lines 25.

## Tools

- `find_product`: Search the shop's products by words (name, brand, type). Returns price, unit, category and the product page.
- `get_product`: Get one product by SKU: price, unit, category, product page and image.
- `get_cart`: Get a cart with its lines.
- `add_to_cart` (changes data, so it asks first): Add a product to a cart. This changes the cart, so it asks first.
- `get_order`: Get an order with its lines: status, delivery type, branch and totals.
- `order_history`: List the orders of a trade account, newest first.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
