# Salsify (sample)

Stands in for: **Salsify: product content, specifications and documents**.

Product descriptions, specifications and documents such as guides and datasheets. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/salsify`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `products` | 100 | real | 0/100 | every field | - |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): products 40.

## Tools

- `get_product_content`: The description, features, keywords and pictures of a product.
- `get_specifications`: The specifications of a product (name, value, unit).
- `get_documents`: The documents kept with a product: guides, datasheets, warranty and supplier links.
- `find_content`: Find products by the words in their content.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
