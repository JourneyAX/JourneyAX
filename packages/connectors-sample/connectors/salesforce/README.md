# Salesforce Sales and Service Cloud (sample)

Stands in for: **Salesforce Sales and Service Cloud: accounts, cases and the support review of online orders**.

Trade accounts, support cases and order reviews. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/salesforce`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `accounts` | 30 | made up | 30/30 | homeBranchCode (a real branch code) | every other field |
| `cases` | 44 | made up | 44/44 | branchCode (a real branch code) | every other field |
| `order_reviews` | 32 | made up | 32/32 | - | every field |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): accounts 25, cases 40, order_reviews 25.

## Tools

- `find_account`: Find trade accounts by name, contact or trade.
- `get_account`: Get one trade account with its cases.
- `list_cases`: List support cases, newest first, for an account or by status or priority.
- `get_case`: Get one support case.
- `create_case` (changes data, so it asks first): Open a new support case for an account. This writes to the service system, so it asks first.
- `order_review_status`: See whether the support team has reviewed an online order, for one order or for an account.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
