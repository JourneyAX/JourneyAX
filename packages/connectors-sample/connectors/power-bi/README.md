# Power BI (sample)

Stands in for: **Power BI: sales and service reports**.

Sales and service reports: which reports exist and their figures. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/power-bi`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `reports` | 6 | made up | 6/6 | - | every field |
| `report_rows` | 437 | made up | 437/437 | dimension in R-TOP-PRODUCTS (a real SKU) | every figure |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): reports 6, report_rows 150.

## Tools

- `list_reports`: The reports that exist, with what each shows.
- `get_report_figures`: The figures of one report, period by period. Narrow by period numbers or by what the figures are split by.
- `report_totals`: The total of a report's figures for each split (for example for each branch).

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
