# Google Analytics (sample)

Stands in for: **Google Analytics 4, Tag Manager and Search Console: web traffic and search**.

Web traffic by page and by day, top searches and conversions by channel. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/google-analytics`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `daily_traffic` | 60 | made up | 60/60 | - | every field |
| `page_traffic` | 41 | real and made up | 41/41 | path, title | views, users, avgEngagementSec, conversions |
| `search_terms` | 32 | real and made up | 32/32 | term | impressions, clicks, ctrPct, avgPosition |
| `channel_conversions` | 30 | made up | 30/30 | - | every field |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): daily_traffic 25, page_traffic 25, search_terms 25, channel_conversions 25.

## Tools

- `traffic_by_page`: Views and visitors for pages of the website, busiest first.
- `top_searches`: The searches that bring people to the website from a search engine, with clicks and position.
- `daily_traffic`: Sessions, visitors and conversions day by day, newest first. Narrow with from and to dates (YYYY-MM-DD).
- `conversion_summary`: Sessions and conversions for each channel (search, direct, email, paid).

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
