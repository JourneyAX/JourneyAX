# Bloomreach (sample)

Stands in for: **Bloomreach: site search and product discovery**.

Site search over the product catalogue, search suggestions and popular searches. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/bloomreach`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `catalog` | 100 | real | 0/100 | every field | - |
| `queries` | 34 | real and made up | 34/34 | query, resultCount, topSkus (found by searching the real catalogue) | count |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): catalog 40, queries 25.

## Tools

- `search`: Search the catalogue the way the shop's site search does. Best matches first.
- `suggestions`: Suggestions for what a shopper has started to type.
- `popular_searches`: The searches shoppers make most, with how many products each finds.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
