# MuleSoft (sample)

Stands in for: **MuleSoft: the integration layer between commerce, CRM and ERP**.

Whether an order has reached the other systems, and the log of integration runs. This is **sample data**: a stand-in that speaks MCP at `POST /mcp/mulesoft`. Generated by `scripts/build-seeds.ts`; do not edit by hand.

## Records

| Collection | Records | Origin | Marked `sample: true` | Real fields (copied from the PlaceMakers data) | Made-up fields |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sync_runs` | 38 | made up | 38/38 | orderId (a sample order) | every other field |

Smallest allowed per collection (a test checks it): sync_runs 25.

## Tools

- `order_sync_status`: Whether an order has been passed on to the other systems, run by run, newest first.
- `integration_run_log`: The log of integration runs, newest first, by flow or status.

Records that are invented, or that mix real and invented values, carry `sample: true`. The people, trade businesses, customer phone numbers and email addresses are made up (emails end in `.test`); any resemblance to a real person or business is chance. Branch names, codes and phone numbers are the ones the PlaceMakers store locator page lists. No customer data was read.
