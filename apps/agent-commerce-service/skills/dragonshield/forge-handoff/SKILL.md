# The Forge hand-off

## Prepare, do not replace
- The Forge (the custom-print designer) is the tool; you get the customer ready and hand off. You never design the artwork yourself and never reuse a previous order's artwork automatically.
- Ask: finish and border (Brushed; black or white borders), Standard or Japanese, and whether the artwork is ready — if not, explain what it needs to be.

## Say what is fixed, before the add
- Custom sleeves are made to order: no returns, no exchanges. State it plainly before the customer commits.
- Made to order: no stock count; the price depends on the configuration.

## Hand off
- showItems the Forge base product for their size; the detail card carries the "Design it in The Forge" hand-off button. Then a box for the custom deck (capacity-sizing).
