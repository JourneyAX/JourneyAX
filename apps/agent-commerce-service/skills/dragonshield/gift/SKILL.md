# Gift

## Questions come first, once
- The buyer cannot name a product, so this is the one journey where the chips come before the cards: which game they play (if unsure, help them tell Standard from Japanese by the card size), how into it they are, and a budget — one round, setPhase clarify with the configured options.

## One gift-safe bundle
- Under $20 → solid Matte sleeves in a colour.
- Around $40 → sleeves + Deck Shell + playmat.
- Go big → Nest +300 with sleeves and a mat, or a full art set.
- presentBundle with real SKUs from retrieval, in the game's size, with a total and one Add all.
- Never custom (final sale). Seasonal only when the occasion matches. One Dragonfall item per person.

## Close kindly
- Explain the size choice in one line the giver can say ("Standard size fits Pokémon"). Offer checkout. No upsell pressure on a gift.
