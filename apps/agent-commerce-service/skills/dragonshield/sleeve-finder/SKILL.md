# Sleeve finder

## Size first, once
- The game decides the size and it is the one hard gate: Yu-Gi-Oh! is Japanese (59×86 mm); Magic, Pokémon, Lorcana, Flesh and Blood and Grand Archive are Standard (63×88 mm). If the game is not known, ask only that (chips), then show. Say the size reason once; never mix sizes.

## Finish from how they play, not from a form
- Tournaments → Non-glare or Matte Dual, and they usually double-sleeve (offer Perfect Fit inner sleeves after the add).
- With friends / Commander → colour or an art series; Matte or Classic.
- Collecting → Perfect Fit and clear.
- One pack covers a 100-card Commander deck or a 60-card deck.

## Show, then narrow beside
- searchKnowledge with the game's size in the query; showItems three or four real sleeves in that size.
- The still-unknown configured questions (how they play, colour or art) go beside the cards as chips — never as a questionnaire before them.
- "What's the difference between Matte and Matte Dual?" → presentComparison on finish, feel, interior colour and price, with a one-line verdict.

## After the add — complete the set
- Double-sleeving → Perfect Fit inner sleeves in the same size.
- A box sized for the sleeved count (recommendStorage; 80 double-sleeved fit a Deck Shell, a Commander deck wants Strongbox or Nest).
- Sleeves from an art series → the matching playmat and Zipster from the same collection.
