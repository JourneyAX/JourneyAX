# JourneyAX — optional speaking scripts for all 12 clips

The MP4 clips are **silent**. These are optional voice-over scripts for recording or a video editor, not audio already included in the files. Read “JAX” as **Jax** and “JourneyAX” as **Journey A-X**. A natural, confident read at roughly 130–145 words per minute should leave a short visual hold at the end of each clip. Do not read the small illustrative/legal footer aloud.

| Clip | Duration | Speaking script |
|---|---:|---|
| 01 — The goal | 12s | Customers do not arrive with a product code. They come with a goal: a room to renovate, a component to source, or a look to find. |
| 02 — The silos | 15s | Today, the answer is scattered across commerce, product data, customer records, search, and service systems. The shopper has to piece it together. The business often has to do the same. |
| 03 — Connect the systems | 18s | JourneyAX connects approved data and actions from the systems a business already uses. Product information, customer context, pricing, stock, and knowledge remain governed at their sources. JAX turns that context into guidance, options, and the next useful action. |
| 04 — Build agents | 13s | A merchant can define specialist agents for discovery, product guidance, and quoting. Each role gets only the approved knowledge, tools, and limits needed for its job. |
| 05 — Business Pack | 15s | The Business Pack is the merchant's playbook: brand voice, knowledge, agents, journeys, rules, cards, and themes. It shapes the experience, while live commerce facts stay in connected systems. |
| 06 — Adaptive journey | 17s | A JourneyAX journey starts with the goal. It remembers what the customer has said, asks for a missing detail only when needed, and moves to a recommendation or action when there is enough context. |
| 07 — Building supplies | 15s | For a laundry renovation, the agent can understand the space, appliances, and storage goal. It asks the next useful question, then guides the customer toward relevant materials, a plan, or a quote. |
| 08 — Manufacturing | 15s | For a manufacturing request, the journey captures fit, quantity, standards, and timing. It checks approved product information and availability, then prepares a suitable option, specification, quote, or human review. |
| 09 — Apparel | 15s | For apparel, the journey considers occasion, preference, and fit. It can curate relevant choices, explain why they match, check availability when connected, and help the shopper build a basket. |
| 10 — Agent in action | 17s | The journey selects a relevant agent and approved capability. It may find products, check live information, read guidance, or prepare an action. The customer receives a reason, not just a result, and a useful next step. |
| 11 — Studio insights | 13s | In JourneyAX Studio, the merchant controls connections, agents, journeys, Business Packs, and insights. Teams can review where customers need help and refine the experience without rebuilding the storefront. |
| 12 — Ownership | 12s | Your customer. Your brand. Your journey. JourneyAX helps turn intent into action, on the channel you own. JourneyAX dot com. |

These lines describe the platform concept, not a statement that every connector or customer action is already live. Confirm deployment-specific features before publishing a narrated marketing version.
