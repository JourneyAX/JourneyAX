# JourneyAX — 60-second LinkedIn cut

This silent cut uses the stronger **Version 2** market hook and a compact version of the **Version 3** product story. It is assembled from motion shots, not a slide deck. The current on-screen copy is intentionally brief; the longer script the team supplied can be used as post copy or optional captions, not voice-over.

| 14–24s | Signals flow from six sources through JAX to useful actions | Connect the business to the journey. |
| 24–29s | Specialist agents activate | The right agent for the job. |
| 29–35s | Business Pack assembles | The merchant controls the playbook. |
| 35–42s | An adaptive path advances | Ask if needed. Act when ready. |
| 42–52s | Journey selects a capability and produces a result | From intent to a useful next action. |
| 52–60s | JourneyAX brand close | Your customer. Your brand. Your journey. |

The rendered cut retains the existing scene typography; the minimal-message column is an edit guide, not a claim that those words are already burned into the MP4. For a public LinkedIn release, the editor should replace/trim labels to match this guide and verify that every integration and action shown is available in the target deployment. The movie is 16:9 and silent. A later social export may add licensed instrumental audio, but no voice is required.

Do not add an unverified reorder, roster, checkout, stock-sync or payment claim just because it appeared in a draft script. The cut shows the platform pattern; actual live features require product-owner sign-off.
