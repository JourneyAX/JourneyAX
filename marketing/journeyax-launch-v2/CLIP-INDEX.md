# JourneyAX animated step clips

These are twelve separate, silent 16:9 H.264 videos. Each is one scene from the 2:57 concept film. Use the architecture and journey clips first in a product introduction; the other clips can be reordered for a particular audience. The source remains `render.mjs` and the cuts are generated by `split-clips.mjs`.

| Clip | Length | What moves on screen |
|---|---:|---|
| `01-01-the-goal.mp4` | 12s | Three customer contexts change: building, manufacturing, retail. |
| `02-02-the-silos.mp4` | 15s | Separate business systems appear and fail to form one customer view. |
| `03-03-connect-the-systems.mp4` | 18s | ERP, CRM, PIM, commerce, search and knowledge signals flow into JAX and onward to useful outcomes. |
| `04-04-build-agents.mp4` | 13s | Discovery, product and quote roles appear and the active specialist changes. |
| `05-05-business-pack.mp4` | 15s | Merchant-controlled voice, knowledge, agents, journeys, rules, cards and themes form a playbook. |
| `06-06-adaptive-journey.mp4` | 17s | Goal, understanding, decision, recommendation and completion light up along an adaptive path. |
| `07-07-building-supplies.mp4` | 15s | Laundry project: understand, clarify only what is missing, then guide a decision. |
| `08-08-manufacturing.mp4` | 15s | Component configuration: requirements, approved product data, option or quote. |
| `09-09-apparel.mp4` | 15s | Fit-and-style request: preferences, options and basket. |
| `10-10-agent-in-action.mp4` | 17s | The journey selects a relevant agent and capability for the customer goal. |
| `11-11-studio-insights.mp4` | 13s | Merchant Studio sections and a conceptual journey path become visible. |
| `12-12-ownership.mp4` | 12s | The customer, brand and journey resolve into the JourneyAX close. |

The diagram is a conceptual architecture, not a claim that every listed integration is available in every deployment. Live price, stock and order facts stay in authoritative systems; the Business Pack controls the merchant experience. Photos, interfaces and journeys are illustrative. No voice or audio is included.

For a final public edit, keep each clip focused on one action and shorten labels before reducing type size. Replace conceptual UI with approved product capture only where the film implies a live feature. Keep the source labels and JourneyAX brand mark editable; do not use a text-to-video model to generate lettering.
